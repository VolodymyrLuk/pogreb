The B2B pricing problem

Prices are fragmented, negotiated, and often hidden behind forms. Manual research is slow, error-prone, and impossible to repeat at scale. Meanwhile, suppliers change offers weekly.
Opaque & dynamic

Many suppliers avoid listing prices publicly or list a single reference price that bears little resemblance to your actual quote when volume, packaging, and delivery terms are applied.

Time sinks

Sourcing teams burn hours jumping between search engines, catalogs, emails, and spreadsheets — only to miss better opportunities that were two clicks away.

Hard to compare

Quotes come in different currencies, units, and INCOTERMS. Normalizing manually invites mistakes and slows decisions when speed wins deals.

Whether you buy commodities by the pallet or sell components by the container, PrismPrice adapts to your workflow.

How PrismPrice works

Purpose-built to turn vague market noise into side-by-side quotes your team can act on immediately.

1

Configure your search

Enter product name, category, location, and optional constraints (MOQ, packaging, certifications). Toggle Automated Communication to allow AI to contact suppliers on your behalf.
2

Multi-engine discovery

The orchestrator fans out queries across multiple search engines and curated supplier directories. Results are ranked for relevance, recency, and completeness.
3

Compliant page capture

Pages are fetched respecting robots policies. The extractor identifies product, unit, currency, and terms. Signals are stored with provenance and timestamps.
4

AI outreach & follow‑ups

Where prices are missing, PrismPrice drafts and sends human‑like emails or webform messages in your tone. It tracks replies, asks clarifying questions, and schedules gentle reminders.
5

Normalization & comparison

Quotes are normalized for currency, unit size, and INCOTERMS (e.g., EXW vs. DDP). You get a sortable table with apples‑to‑apples comparisons and quick filters.
6

Collaborate & decide

Share a link, add comments, and export to CSV or your CRM. One click to accept, decline, or request a better offer using pre‑approved negotiation templates.
